Food · presets, hunger that drains, and a recipe menu

Two phones, both interactive. Left: the You tab with quick presets and a game-style hunger bar that drops through the day (drag the time slider to watch it). Right: Recipes as a menu, with tabs by meal, sections by main food, and Random / Choose for me.

You tab · presets + draining hunger

Quick presets: a single row of small chips (Banana ~105, Yogurt ~150, Nuts ~170, Toast + PB ~300, Sandwich ~450, Takeout ~900). One tap logs it; long-press to edit. Hunger bar: starts a bit low when you wake, drains about one drumstick every 70 minutes, and each meal refills it based on its calories, like in the game. Under 3 drumsticks it wobbles.

Recipes · a menu

Tabs by meal: Breakfast · Mains · Shakes & drinks · Snacks · Lessons · Dogs. Inside each, dishes are grouped by main food (Chicken, Beef, Seafood, Eggs…) like a restaurant menu, with time and est. calories. 🎲 Random spins through the tab; Choose for me picks smartly: this week's theme first, something you haven't eaten lately, and enough protein.
