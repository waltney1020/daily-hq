Streak + Rewards · interactive mock

  • The 🔥 chip opens this page. Four tabs: Streak, Coach, Store, History.
  • You earn coins 🪙 for discipline: each task, clearing your day, hitting water and meal goals, beating a smoke urge, logging trades and journal entries, plus big bonuses at streak milestones.
  • Tiers: your streak sets your tier (Bronze 3 days, Silver 7, Gold 30, Diamond 100). Bigger rewards unlock at higher tiers, so you can't just save up; you have to stay consistent.
  • Store: rewards to do (gaming hour, cheat meal, sleep in, day off) and to buy (pulled from your Things to buy list, priced by its $). Tap Buy to spend coins; add your own rewards.
  • Coach: looks at the last 30 days and tells you why streaks break: how often you miss by just 1 task, which task it usually is, when misses happen (e.g. evenings), and why, using your actual schedule. Each insight has one-tap fixes like "Move to 5:00 PM", "Remind me 10 min before" or "Make optional on weekends". The Streak tab shows the top insight up front.
  • Try buying something on the Store tab, and the fix buttons on Coach.
