  • Clean mode: a professional meal tracker: calories, a bar per meal, and "last ate 3 h ago".
  • Switch with the little button on the right of the bar (or the buttons below).
  • Tap the bar and it grows into a full Food page: calories and protein, quick-add for your meal-plan meals, today's meals, and a "remind me if I haven't eaten" switch.
